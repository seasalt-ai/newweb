## Available Care Services by County/City:

### Taipei

Taipei City provides a range of care services targeting the diverse needs of elderly people. The city offers a 24-hour emergency help hotline: 1999, ensuring elderly people can get help anytime they need it. Additionally, Taipei City has multiple community centers and support organizations providing daily assistance, social activities and health services.

For more information about Taipei City Social Welfare Bureau, please visit [Taipei City Social Welfare Bureau](https://dosw.gov.taipei/).

### New Taipei

New Taipei City not only provides basic elderly care services, but also has a 24-hour service hotline for emergencies: 02-29603456. The city's Social Welfare Bureau also provides psychological support and health consultation, helping elderly people cope with various challenges.

For more information about New Taipei City Social Welfare Bureau, please visit [New Taipei City Social Welfare Bureau](https://www.sw.ntpc.gov.tw/).

### Taichung

In Taichung City, the Social Welfare Bureau plays an important role in supporting the city's elderly residents. Elderly people can get help through the bureau's services, which include consultation, home care and social worker complaint hotline: 04-22289111. The city also emphasizes the importance of mental health and well-being, providing resources such as dementia care hotline: 0800-474-580 and face-to-face support.

For more information about Taichung City Social Welfare Bureau, please visit [Taichung City Social Welfare Bureau](https://www.society.taichung.gov.tw/880452/post).

### Taoyuan

Taoyuan City also values elderly care, providing various medical and social support services. The city's 24-hour emergency help hotline: 03-3333001, is designed to provide elderly people with quick and effective help, ensuring immediate support when needed.

For more information about Taoyuan City Social Welfare Bureau, please visit [Taoyuan City Social Welfare Bureau](https://sab.tycg.gov.tw/).

### Tainan

Tainan City provides diverse care services for elderly people, including health checkups, daily life assistance and social activities. The city's elderly care hotline: 06-2991111, is specifically set up for elderly people, providing round-the-clock help and support.

For more information about Tainan City Social Welfare Bureau, please visit [Tainan City Social Welfare Bureau](https://sab.tainan.gov.tw/).

### Kaohsiung

Kaohsiung City is committed to improving the quality of life for elderly citizens. The city provides comprehensive service coverage including healthcare, social support and emergency hotline: 07-3368333. Kaohsiung's elderly people can rely on these resources to address emergency needs and ongoing care, ensuring they receive the attention and support they deserve.

For more information about Kaohsiung City Social Welfare Bureau, please visit [Kaohsiung City Social Welfare Bureau](https://socbu.kcg.gov.tw/).
