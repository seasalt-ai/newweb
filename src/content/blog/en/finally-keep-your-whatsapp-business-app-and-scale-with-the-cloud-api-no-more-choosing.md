### The Landscape: Choosing Your Growth Tier

To understand why this is such a breakthrough, let’s look at the WhatsApp Business landscape in 2026. Until now, you had to fit into one of three rigid boxes:

#### 1. The Growth Foundation: WhatsApp Business App (Free)

This is where almost every business starts. It’s perfect for solo entrepreneurs.

* **Reach:** Limited. Broadcast lists are restricted to 256 contacts.
* **Multi-User Support:** Capped at **5 devices total** (1 phone + 4 web browsers).
* **Automation:** Very basic (Greeting messages only).
* **Verification:** No verification badge.

#### 2. The Middle Ground: WhatsApp Business Premium

Designed for growing SMBs who need a bit more room but aren't ready for full enterprise tech.

* **Focus:** Web presence with custom business URLs (e.g., `https://wa.me/yourbusiness`).
* **Multi-User Support:** Expanded to **10 devices**.
* **Verification:** You become eligible for verification, but automation capabilities remain limited.

#### 3. The Scale Tier: Business API (WABA) via SeaX

This is the highest tier, designed for businesses ready for global reach and efficiency.

* **Reach:** **Unlimited contacts** and global broadcasting.
* **Multi-User Support:** **Unlimited agents** working via a centralized shared inbox.
* **Advanced Features:**
* **Green Tick Verification:** Establish immediate trust.
* **Advanced AI & Chatbots:** Capable of **80% Automated FAQ Resolution**, handling the vast majority of repetitive inquiries without human intervention.



### The 2026 Strategic Pivot

Historically, moving from Tier 2 (Premium) to Tier 3 (API) meant sacrificing the ease of Tier 1. You gained unlimited agents and AI, but you lost the ability to pull your phone out of your pocket and reply to a VIP client instantly via the native app.

With **WhatsApp Coexistence**, that trade-off is gone.

**Visualizing Coexistence:** Imagine two phones—one showing the Business App icon and one showing the API icon—both connected to a single phone number.

With Coexistence on SeaX, you get:

1. **Unlimited Scale:** You get the power of the API (Tier 3), including Meta's new per-message pricing model for Marketing, Utility, and Authentication templates.
2. **Native Convenience:** You keep the Business App (Tier 1) on your phone for personal oversight.
3. **Maximum Efficiency:** You can deploy SeaChat AI agents to resolve that **80% of FAQs**, while your human team handles complex issues on the SeaX dashboard.

### How It Works: The Tri-Sync System

When you enable Coexistence on Seasalt.ai, your business communications enter a "Tri-Sync" state. Data synchronizes in real-time across three critical touchpoints:

1. **Your Physical Phone:** The native WhatsApp Business App remains active.
2. **The Office:** The **SeaX Web Dashboard**, where your support team manages the shared inbox.
3. **The Field:** The **SeaX Mobile App**, allowing your staff to reply on the go without needing access to your personal device.
