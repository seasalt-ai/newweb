With SeaMeet, you can:

- Get instant written transcripts of meetings
- Automatically summarize key points
- Identify tasks and actions
- Analyze sentiment and engagement

These features help teams to:

1. Save time on note-taking
2. Improve task follow-up
3. Enhance transparency and accountability
4. Accelerate decision-making

To get started:

1. Install the SeaMeet browser extension
2. Join a Google Meet or Teams meeting as usual
3. SeaMeet will automatically start recording and analyzing the conversation

Pro Tip: Use the "Search History" feature to quickly find information from previous meetings.
